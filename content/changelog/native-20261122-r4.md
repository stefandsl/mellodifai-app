# Publish QA gating, Suno artwork import, and branded sign-in mail

Publishing now holds renders that fail quality checks, imported tracks keep their embedded cover art, and sign-in emails carry MellodifAI branding.

## What changed

- Held publish approval cards and upload handoffs when a video render fails QA, showing the reason in Pubblica and the agent guide.
- Preserved embedded cover artwork and lyrics when importing Suno MP3 tracks.
- Branded the magic-link sign-in email with an Italian subject line.
- Hardened the native service supervisor so a fast-crashing hosted child process does not take down its supervisor, and rejected unsupported legacy import bundles.
