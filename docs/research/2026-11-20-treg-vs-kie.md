# treg.to against kie.ai (T21, Parallax P6)

Date 2026-11-20. Public pages only, no account, no paid call. Full table with every price and the terms comparison: `E:/mellodifai/research/2026-11-20/t21-treg-vs-kie.md`.

| Need | kie.ai | treg.to |
|---|---|---|
| Suno generate / extend / cover | $0.06 per call (12 credits) | not offered |
| Suno replace section | about $0.025 (5 credits) | not offered |
| Stem separation | $0.05 vocals, $0.25 multi-stem | not offered |
| Other song model | none | Google Lyria 3.5, $0.08 per song |
| Nano Banana Pro image | from $0.09 | $0.03-$0.301 by route (only reAPI is cheaper) |
| Seedance 2.5 video | from $0.085 per second | $0.41-$24 per success, duration not stated |
| Credits | never expire, refunds "supported if needed", failed tasks free | prepaid balance, no refund policy, $1 free credit |
| Liability | "as is", no cap stated | "as is", cap greater of 12 months' fees or US$100 |

Decision: no switch. treg.to has none of the Suno operations mellodifai pays for, its song model costs more per song, and no saving was measured (the rule is "no switch without a measured saving"). Revisit if treg adds a Suno-class song endpoint below $0.06, or if M3 enables a paid image route and the owner approves a capped side-by-side test.
