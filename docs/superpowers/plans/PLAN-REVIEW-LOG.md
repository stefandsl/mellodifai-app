- **High** — [supabase/migrations/20261031000005_pipeline_hardening.sql:81](/C:/Users/Asus/Documents/mellodifai/supabase/migrations/20261031000005_pipeline_hardening.sql:81): privacy confirmations are not idempotent. Double-clicking or selecting two options can issue multiple YouTube privacy calls with a surprising final value. Fix: serialize on publication state and use a deterministic confirmation/version key, rejecting a second active privacy job.
